## 05 — Change One Thing at a Time

> Remove the changes that didn’t do what you expected. They probably did something you didn’t expect

* If you change something, and it doesn’t fix it, change it back
* Simple rule: Change, test, reverse

^
— Imagine trying to find the cause behind something that doesn’t turn on
— I check the cord, and swap it with another one
— I check the contacts, but don’t verify that I’ve put it back properly
— You find a blown fuse, and replace it
— It still doesn’t work, because you didn’t install the cord properly
— And/or you replaced a good cord with a bad one
