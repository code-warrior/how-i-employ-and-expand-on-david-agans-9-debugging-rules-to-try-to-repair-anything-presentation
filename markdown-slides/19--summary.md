## Summary

1. Understand the system (read the manual)
2. Make it fail (look, focus, verify)
3. Quit thinking and look (start looking once you’ve thought hard)
4. Divide and conquer (keep dividing in two)
5. Change one thing at a time (change, test, reverse)
6. Keep an audit trail (document in writing, images, and/or movies)
7. Check the plug (is it plugged in?)
8. Get a fresh view (get help)
9. If you didn’t fix it, it ain’t fixed (open/close is not a fix)
10. Work cleanly and neatly (organizationess is awesomeness)
11. Use the right tool (if the tool don’t fit, don’t force it)
12. Leave a note (“I was here, and this is what I did”)
13. Leave it better than you found it (in: good; return: better)
