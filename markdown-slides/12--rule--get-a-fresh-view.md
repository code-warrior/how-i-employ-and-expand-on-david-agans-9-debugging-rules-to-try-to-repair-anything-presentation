## 08 — Get a Fresh View

> There are at least three reasons to ask for help ...: a fresh view, expertise, and experience

* Like the rubber ducky situation in software engineering, explaining the issue to someone else provides a different perspective
* If possible, ask someone who knows more
* Find someone who specializes in what you’re doing
* Report symptoms, not theories; otherwise, you’ve tainted the listener’s indifferent position

^
— When I ask for help, I never tell the other fixer what I think is happening, only what symptoms I see
— When someone asks for my help, I always start with, “Don’t tell me what you think, tell me what you see”
