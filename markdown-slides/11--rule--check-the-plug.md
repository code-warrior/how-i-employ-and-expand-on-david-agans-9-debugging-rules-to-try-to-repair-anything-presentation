## 07 — Check the Plug

> Obvious assumptions are often wrong

* “Don’t start at square three”
* It may be obvious, but never trust your assumptions: Is it plugged in?
* **Tip**: If you work with mains power (120V, 15A in the US), keep a fire extinguisher nearby

^
— True story: With a friend back in the late 1990s, we were both working on his computer
— It didn’t work at his place, so he brought it to mine
— We tried for hours and gave up
— He drove home, then a few hours later, drove back: “Don’t tell anyone, but neither you nor I ever checked for power”
