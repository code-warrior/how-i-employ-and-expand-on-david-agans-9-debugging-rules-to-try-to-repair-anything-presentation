## 01 — Understand the System

> You need a working knowledge of what the system is supposed to do, how it’s designed, and, in some cases, why it was designed that way

* In other words, RTM, or read the manual
* The crude version being, RTFM
* Agans goes on to say, “If you don’t understand some part of the system, that always seems to be where the problem is”

^
— I always ask if the guest has the manual
— Or I’ll look up the model number to see if a manual is online, or a video tutorial
— I once helped someone who said that her disco light was faulty: Every time the light encountered strong vibrations from a speaker, it blinked
— We couldn’t find the manual, but, when I opened it, I found a sound detection sensor
— What the guest perceived as a bug was actually a feature
— She would have known this if she had read the 20-word manual
