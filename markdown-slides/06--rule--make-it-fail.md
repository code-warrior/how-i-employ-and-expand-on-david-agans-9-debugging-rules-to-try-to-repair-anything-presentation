## 02 — Make It Fail

[.build-lists: not_first]

> There are three reasons for trying to make it fail: So you can look at it ... So you can focus on the cause ... So you can tell if you’ve fixed it

* **Look**: Make it fail enough so you know why it fails. Sometimes, easy: No power to the device, no matter how hard I try. Other times, more difficult: The leak in the trunk of the car only happens when it rains…_some_ of the time
