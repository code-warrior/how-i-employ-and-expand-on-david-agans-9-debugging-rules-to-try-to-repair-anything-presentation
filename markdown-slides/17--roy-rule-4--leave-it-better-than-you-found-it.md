## Leave it Better Than You Found It (Roy Rule 4)

> Always leave the thing you’re fixing better than you found it

* I’m a thief; this isn’t my rule (thanks, Boy Scouts and Uncle Bob)
* We use this in software and hardware engineering
* This isn’t always possible, but it should *always* start with how you open something
* If you’ve progressed, but it’s not fixed, it’s in better condition for the next person
* In: good; return: better

^
— I see so often people opening stuff quickly before thinking of the available tools, inspecting where the seems are, and how gently to open something
