## 03 — Quit Thinking and Look

> If you guess at how something is failing, you often fix something that isn’t the bug

* Start looking once you’ve thought hard
* You may take guesses, but they’ll make more sense when you understand the system/item
* And guesses are only useful to focus your search
* Guessing is fine if it’s both very likely _and_ easy to “fix”
* Don’t be afraid to do the hard work, if that’s what’s required

^
— Fix should be “fix”
