## 04 — Divide and Conquer

> It’s hard for a bug to keep hiding when its hiding place keeps getting cut in half

* In essence, narrow your search in half each time
* Keep dividing in two
* Guess a number between 1 and 100, inclusive, in 7 guesses

^
— Consider finding a breach in a power cable without a voltage tester; you’re using a needle probe
— You’d half each successive time
