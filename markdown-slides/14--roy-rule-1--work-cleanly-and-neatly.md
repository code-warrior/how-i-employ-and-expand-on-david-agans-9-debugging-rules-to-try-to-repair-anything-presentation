## Work Cleanly and Neatly (Roy Rule 1)

> Work cleanly and neatly: Professional cooks work in — and on — clean surfaces, as do professional mechanics, etc. So should you

* Cleanliness and organizationess is godliness and awesomeness
* The more cleanly and neatly you work, the more time you can dedicate to the thought process of repair
* If, while you’re working, you find that it’s stressful to find things, then your train of thought is interrupted
